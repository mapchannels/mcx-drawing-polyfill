Circle & Rectangle Tools

Use the toolbar to draw. For the circle and rectangle tools, press and drag to draw (the map stops panning while these tools are active — switch back to the hand to pan). Once drawn, shapes are editable: drag the handles to resize, drag the body to move, or type exact dimensions in metres below. Click any shape to re-select it, and click empty map (or Save & deselect) to lock it and hide the handles.
